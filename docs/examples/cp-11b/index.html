One chain, end to end: CP-11b

A worked example: one finding followed from the documents to the catalogue, as the README gives it. The finding on the Findings page; its question Q1.

  1. Documents. All 12 CPMP ISAs have an eGFR exclusion with the same CKD-EPI formula at Visit 1 or Visit 2. The threshold depends on the compound: less than 70 ml/min/1.73 m² for LY3016859 (OA01 [1004], BP01 [2003], NP01 [3003]), 60 for LY3556050 ([x030]), and 30 for LY3526318 and LY3857210 ([x052], [x077]) (usdm-master-protocol/output_cpmp/step5/criteria.csv). OA01's number is shifted by its extra criterion [1003].
  2. Proposal. The crosswalk pairs criteria with similar wording (difflib ratio ≥ 0.85) or in the same slot of the ISA numbering. Claude proposed a decision per pair, with a pattern: P1 "eGFR threshold differs (same rule, different value)", P3 and P8 for variants that differ only in wording.
  3. Decision. Kerstin decided each pair in cpmp-design-modules/sources/crosswalk_decisions.csv: the 12 P1 pairs are kept apart; the wording-only variants are merged (11 P3 pairs for < 30, 1 P8 same-slot pair for < 70). Three < 70 / < 60 pairs, kept apart in an earlier review group, are not P1 and are not counted: 27 eGFR rows in the decision file, 12 in this finding.
  4. Build. cpmp-design-modules/scripts/01_build_modules.py merges only pairs decided "merge", and logs every pair kept apart (output/build_log.csv).
  5. Finding. CP-11b in evidence-matrix/sources/findings.csv, with the expected value "eGFR values 30/60/70". The selector cp11b() in evidence-matrix/scripts/01_build_matrix.py reads the 12 P1 decisions and both texts of each pair, and extracts the thresholds. That gives 12 evidence rows, status decision by Kerstin.
  6. Check. Observed "eGFR values 30/60/70" equals the expected value: pass (output/findings_checked.csv). A changed decision or threshold stops the build.
  7. Catalogue. Q1, row CP-11b, with the answer, the check and the status (competency-questions/output/catalogue.md).

What it shows: text identity cannot say "the same rule with a different value". A design model needs the rule as one component with a parameter that varies by compound.
