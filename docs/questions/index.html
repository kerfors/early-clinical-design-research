The questions

The work is organised by ten competency questions. Each is answered per case by SPARQL queries over the design modules and by findings with document evidence. The catalogue gives the question, the case questions and findings under it, the answer, how it was checked, and its status: competency-questions/output/catalogue.md.

Each card counts what the catalogue holds per case: findings with document evidence, case questions answered by SPARQL over the design modules, and questions still open; "both" is a question asked of the two cases together. Earlier answers (before the modules) are on the question pages.
