CPMP: the documents

Lilly Chronic Pain Master Protocol (H0P-MC-CPMP). One master protocol; one disease-state addendum (DSA) per disease, not public; one intervention-specific appendix (ISA) per compound and disease, each registered on ClinicalTrials.gov under its own NCT ID with the ISA posted as its protocol.

Solid box: posted document. Dashed box: a layer the ISAs refer to, not public. The grid is read from each ISA's section 5 sentence naming the master protocol and its DSA (grid.csv); registry data from the ClinicalTrials.gov records (cpmp_studies.json, query in QUERY.txt).
