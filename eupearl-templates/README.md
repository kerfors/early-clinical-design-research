# EU-PEARL templates as data

The five EU-PEARL platform-trial templates (25 April 2023) read as documents with their own structure:
Master Protocol Template (MPT, V4, based on TransCelerate CPT v8.0), Intervention Specific Appendix (ISA, V4),
Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP, V3), Data Monitoring Committee charter (DMC, V1) and the CTR cover-letter
guidance (V4). Source: https://eu-pearl.eu/eu-pearls-suite-of-master-protocol-templates/

The .docx files are not in this repo. Download them into `templates/`; the notebook finds them by name pattern.
Everything below comes from the .docx files. Nothing is inferred.

- **Sections** (`sections.csv`): the heading tree with computed numbers and a key per section, and per section
  the number of paragraphs in each text category. MPT 133, ISA 100, SAP 42, DMC 46 sections.
- **Text categories** from the templates' own conventions: `instruction` (paragraph style), `common`,
  `suggested`, `example` and `sample` (start and end markers), and guidance text in the DMC charter.
  Marker anomalies are reported, not corrected (`marker_events.csv`, `unreliable_block_category.csv`).
- **EU-PEARL changes to CPT v8.0**: yellow-highlighted characters per section.
- **TOC check** (`toc_check.csv`): computed section numbers against each document's TOC. 252 match, 34 differ,
  16 are below the TOC depth, 6 are not in the TOC. The DMC TOC is stale (known, left unresolved).
- **Field labels** (`field_labels.csv`): title-page and synopsis items such as `Sponsor Name:`, the CPT
  counterpart of the ICH M11 title-page elements. MPT 20, ISA 17.
- **MPT ↔ ISA alignment** (`mpt_isa_alignment.csv`): sections matched on the exact normalised title path, with
  no fuzzy matching. 90 matched, 43 in the MPT only, 10 in the ISA only. For each ISA section, three relation
  flags (refers to master, extends master, either location) are set only where an explicit phrase occurs, with
  the sentence kept as evidence.

Not in the public repo: `paragraphs.csv` and `tables.csv` (the full template text). The notebook re-creates them.

Run: `eupearl_template_extraction.ipynb` from this folder (generated by `../scripts/build_notebook.py`).
Needs pandas and python-docx.
